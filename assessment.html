ENGLISH PLACEMENT ASSESSMENT

Discover your English level.

Before we build your learning path, let's understand where your English is today. This assessment helps identify your current strengths and areas for development.

Estimated Time 30–40 minutes
Assessment Areas 5+ English Skills
Framework CEFR-Aligned

What will be assessed?

The assessment evaluates several important areas of practical English ability.

Grammar Accuracy, structure and usage
Vocabulary Range, meaning and appropriate usage
Reading Comprehension and interpretation
Writing Clarity, organization and language control
Listening Understanding spoken English
Speaking Evaluated separately with your instructor

Speaking is evaluated separately

Your online assessment covers the written and receptive skills first. A separate speaking evaluation with Marco will assess fluency, pronunciation, interaction, vocabulary and communicative ability.

Ready to find your level?

Set aside approximately 30–40 minutes, find a quiet place, and answer each question as naturally and honestly as possible.
